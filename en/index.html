ABOUT DELTARUNE

DELTARUNE is an 2D RPG idealized by Toby "Radiation" Fox, being the second game of his career after the huge success of Undertale. Both games involves same characters and their counterparts, being DELTARUNE an parallel universe. It has strong influences from the Mother saga (also known as EarthBound), SNES game produced by Shigesato Itoi.
