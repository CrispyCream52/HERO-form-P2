ABOUT HIM

Paige was an lightner and one of the heroes of an old prophecy version that was lost to time. He had holy magic proeficiency and often served as a cleric, specially focusing on being the team's support.

Despite it's own nature, Paige was considered as a leader amongst the party, guiding the team to victory.
